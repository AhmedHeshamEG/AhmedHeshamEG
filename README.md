## Ahmed Hesham

ML engineer, studying Mathematical Sciences for AI at Sapienza University of Rome. I build models of the physical
world, explain science on YouTube, and make tools I wish existed.

### Research

**[Craton](https://github.com/AhmedHeshamEG/craton)**: a foundation model for the solid Earth, built the way language
models are built. Every measurement (a seismogram window, a day of GPS, a tidal stress, a line in a medieval chronicle)
becomes one kind of token; one masked objective; a benchmark suite that only lets the model get better. Trains on a
6 GB laptop GPU. [Project page](https://ahmedheshameg.github.io/craton/)

**[A Non-Extensive Statistical Framework for Exoplanet Demographics](https://github.com/AhmedHeshamEG/Tsallis-Exoplanet-Demographics)**:
Tsallis statistics applied to the Kepler DR25 stellar catalogue. A q-exponential fitted by maximum likelihood
(L-BFGS-B), with profile-likelihood scans and bootstrap uncertainties. Preprint in preparation.
[Calculator](https://huggingface.co/spaces/realahmedh/Tsallis-Exoplanet-Calculator)

**[AI Exoplanet Predictor](https://github.com/AhmedHeshamEG/AI-Exoplanet-Predictor)**: which star should a survey
look at first? An isolation forest trained on confirmed planet hosts. NASA Space Apps Challenge, with Guido Sbrogiò.

**[WC2026 Predictor](https://github.com/AhmedHeshamEG/wc2026-predictor)**: the 2026 World Cup as a probability
distribution. Elo ratings and a Poisson scoreline model in one ensemble, then the 48-team tournament simulated
thousands of times. [Dashboard](https://ahmedheshameg.github.io/wc2026-predictor/)
